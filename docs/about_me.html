The problem

Security reviews arrive late and audits arrive in a rush

Where it breaks

Scans are set up by hand, results sit in separate tools, and evidence is collected the week before an audit. Findings reach engineers after the release is already planned.

What I build instead

Self service automation that runs scans on every change, normalizes the results and shows them in one dashboard, so evidence already exists when someone asks for it.

Security that ships with the code. Compliance that runs on every commit, not once a year.

What you get

An architect who treats compliance as an engineering problem

  • Security gates in every pipeline

    Static, dynamic, interactive, dependency and container scans run inside CI/CD, so teams see findings before release.

  • Audit evidence on demand

    A Twistlock scanner for product Kubernetes clusters produced OS and application level reports that helped teams through SOC 2 audits.

  • One view of security posture

    Aegis brings vulnerability tracking, compliance scorecards, PSIRT, ITSS and SPbD boards into one place.

  • Legal and open source checks automated

    CSAR automation turned a manual legal step into a delivery check for Abbreviated Pedigree Reviews, Certificates of Originality and OSSC clearances.

  • Architecture that holds at scale

    A queue backed broker onboards 1,400+ products a day into IBM Concert with zero data loss.

FAQ

Questions before you write

What does a software architect with a security focus do for my team?

I design systems where scanning, policy checks and evidence collection are part of the delivery pipeline. At IBM that means self service tooling for static, dynamic, interactive, dependency and container testing, with results in one dashboard.

Which security tools have you automated?

OWASP ZAP for dynamic API testing, SonarQube Enterprise for static analysis, Mend for open source and SAST findings, Twistlock for container and cluster scanning, IAST for runtime testing, and CSAR for open source and legal clearance.

Have you supported compliance audits?

My Twistlock scanner for product Kubernetes clusters has been used by teams going through SOC 2 audits. I also work on the Aegis boards for PSIRT, ITSS and Security and Privacy by Design. I cannot speak for how your own organization is certified.

Which technologies do you build with?

React, Next.js, JavaScript, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Spring Boot and Rust, with PostgreSQL, MongoDB and MySQL. For delivery I use Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, OpenShift, Terraform and Jenkins.

Can you lead a team as well as design the system?

Yes. At Syncfusion I led 8 developers and 1 tester and delivered to timeline without cutting quality. At IBM I mentor engineers across teams on technical depth and career growth.

What is the best way to reach you?

Email is the fastest route. Describe the system, the control or audit you are working toward, and the timeline. I will tell you plainly whether it is a fit.
